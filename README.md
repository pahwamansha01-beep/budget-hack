# 💸 BudgetHack | Student-First FinTech Platform

> **Smart, predictive allowance tracking tailored for campus living.**  
> Built to bridge the gap between complex budgeting apps and real student financial realities.

---

## 📌 Overview

**BudgetHack** is a multi-view FinTech web application specifically engineered for college and university students. Traditional expense trackers are generic and reactive—they only tell you that you've overspent after the money is already gone. 

BudgetHack introduces a **Predictive Money Runway** engine and an **Emergency Broke Alert Hub** that uses daily spending velocity to forecast exactly when a student will run out of cash, while providing actionable, micro-level spending adjustments to make funds last until the end of the month.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎓 1. Student Portal Authentication & Persona Modes
- **Student ID Login:** Access using a college Roll Number and portal password.
- **Dual Persona Workflows:**
  - **Hosteller Mode:** Focuses on mess dues, shared flat/hostel rent, roommate bill splitting, bulk stationery, and late-night canteen orders.
  - **Day-Scholar Mode:** Tailored for daily commute tracking (metro, bus, fuel), daily lunch allowances, and semester transport passes.

### 📉 2. Predictive "Money Runway" & Burn Velocity
- Uses daily spending velocity to calculate your cash runway: *"At this rate, you will run out by the 22nd."*
- Proactive suggestions to adjust habits before hitting a zero balance.

### 🚨 3. Dedicated Broke Alert Center
- Dedicated emergency view with real-time zero-balance countdowns.
- Configurable minimum-balance danger thresholds.
- **AI Cash Survival Directives:** Actionable, ranked steps to freeze discretionary spend and settle peer balances immediately.

### 🧾 4. Campus Expense Tracker & Group Splitter
- Category-specific tracking (Canteen, Stationery, Travel, Mess).
- Built-in roommate bill splitter for shared expenses like WiFi, late-night food deliveries, and room amenities.

### 🎯 5. Micro-Savings & Spending Caps
- Visual progress rings for student micro-goals (e.g., *Goa Semester Trip*, *ANC Earphones*, *Hackathon Fees*).
- Hard spending limits with visual alert meters before limits are breached.

### 🏆 6. Gamified Social Leaderboard & Financial Health Score
- Proprietary **Financial Health Score (300–900)** based on savings consistency and impulse control.
- Anonymous peer/hostel block leaderboards and discipline badges (*"7-Day No-Impulse Streak"*).

### 🏷️ 7. Student Perks & Deals Directory
- Direct access to verified student discounts including UNiDAYS, Apple Education, Amazon Prime Student, and Adobe Creative Cloud.

### 🌓 8. Theme Flexibility
- Instant Light Mode / Dark Mode toggle designed for study sessions at any time of day.
